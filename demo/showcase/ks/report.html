\[\dot{u} = - u u_{x} - u_{xx} - u_{xxxx}\]
Summary. The dataset is a single 1-D periodic chaotic field u(x,t) on a domain of length about 100.5, and the recovered law is the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation, u_t = −u·u_x − u_xx − u_xxxx: nonlinear advection plus long-wave anti-diffusion plus short-wave hyperdiffusion, all with unit coefficients.

Physical interpretation. −u_xx is negative (anti-)diffusion: it destabilises long wavelengths and injects energy into the system. −u_xxxx is hyperdiffusion: it damps short wavelengths. Together they select an intrinsic length scale of about 2π√2. −u·u_x is Burgers-type nonlinear advection, written in conservative form as −(u²/2)_x; it moves energy from unstable to damped scales, sustains spatiotemporal chaos, and conserves the spatial mean. This is the standard model for flame-front instabilities, thin-film flow and phase turbulence.

Caveats
  • The automated assessment reports low confidence. It says u_xx is not significant, strongly favours adding a u term (dBIC −1860), and lists five equally good models. All of this comes from the weak-form fit. That fit returned coefficients shrunk by a factor of 3–5 and confuses u with u_xx, so it contradicts both the strong-form fits and the direct spectral time-stepping check. Before trusting these flags, inspect the weak-form normalisation and test-function scaling.
  • The toolkit's rollout valid time is only 1.2 time units (3 samples), and its predictability horizon is 0.8. The system is chaotic, so long-horizon pointwise agreement is not expected. Still, this short horizon is hard to reconcile with the ~1e-7 one-step errors from the custom spectral solver; the toolkit's integrator or its differentiation may be the limiting factor.
  • Only one trajectory is available. The u ↔ u_xx degeneracy, and any small higher-order correction such as u·u_xxx at about 0.5%, cannot be fully ruled out from this single run.
  • The data appear to be clean simulation output (this is the PySINDy tutorial KS dataset). Robustness to measurement noise has therefore not been tested.

Intuition (pre-analysis, before any fitting)

  • [high] the spatial mean of u is conserved: the right-hand side is a total derivative (flux form), e.g. (u^2)_x, u_xx, u_xxx; no source terms like u or u^2
  • [low] u is statistically symmetric under u -> -u: nonlinearities are likely odd (u^3) or of advective type; quadratic sources are unlikely
